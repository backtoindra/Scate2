Welcome to SCATE

South Carolina Association for Telangana Empowerment (SCATE) is a community organization dedicated to bringing together the Telangana community across South Carolina.

SCATE gives families, professionals, youth and community members a place to connect, celebrate our culture, preserve our heritage, serve our neighbors and build lasting relationships — from Columbia and Charleston to Greenville and everywhere between.

Our Mission

“SCATE's mission is to empower the Telangana community and the broader South Carolina community through education, cultural preservation, youth development, community service, charitable initiatives, professional development, and programs that promote Telangana's language, arts, heritage, and traditions.”

Cultural preservation

Festivals, music, dance and the Telugu language carried forward here at home.

Education

Language classes, scholarships and learning support for students of every age.

Youth development

Leadership, performance and volunteering opportunities for the next generation.

Community service

Food drives, blood drives and local partnerships across South Carolina.

Charitable initiatives

Relief and support efforts for causes in Telangana and in our own communities.

Professional development

Networking, mentoring and career guidance among members and newcomers.

Celebrating Telangana

From the heart of Telangana to our home in South Carolina.

Charminar

Hyderabad's four minarets, standing since 1591.

Ramappa Temple

The Kakatiya masterpiece at Palampet, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Warangal Fort

Kakatiya Kala Thoranam, the emblem carried on Telangana's state seal.

Tank Bund & Hussain Sagar

The lakeside Buddha statue rising from the water.

Bathukamma

The flower festival of Telangana, celebrated each autumn.

Bonalu

Decorated pots carried in procession each Ashada.

Telangana cuisine

Hyderabadi biryani, sarva pindi, jonna roti and sakinalu.

Arts & handlooms

Pochampally ikat, Gadwal sarees and Nirmal paintings.
